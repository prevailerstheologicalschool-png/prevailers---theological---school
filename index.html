Who We Are

A school for faith, truth, excellence and service.

Prevailers Theological School is a non-denominational open and distance learning institution dedicated to accessible theological education, Christian leadership development, research and practical ministry preparation.

Established on 22 December 2023, the School exists to provide opportunities for people aspiring to acquire sound theological knowledge and prepare for effective Christian service.

Our vision is to produce Christian leaders who build the Body of Christ and become light in the darkness of the present age.

Our Mission

To provide and raise a God-fearing generation committed to serving humanity through sound theological knowledge.

Through teaching, research and manpower development, the School seeks to contribute to reformation and growth in the Body of Christ.
