WHO WE ARE

A school for faith, truth, excellence and service.

Prevailers Theological School is an open and distance learning institution dedicated to accessible theological education, Christian leadership development, research and practical ministry preparation.

Established on 22 December 2023, the School exists to provide greater opportunities for people aspiring to acquire sound theological knowledge and prepare for effective Christian service.
